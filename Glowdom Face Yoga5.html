Discover the practice

What is Face Yoga?

Face yoga is a natural wellbeing practice that brings together facial exercises, self-massage, acupressure and relaxation.

Think of it as a workout for your face! Just as we exercise our bodies, we can learn to engage, relax and become more aware of our facial muscles.

At Glowdom, we combine energising exercises with calming techniques, helping you develop a simple self-care routine that fits into everyday life.

No previous experience is needed — just a little curiosity and a willingness to give yourself some time.

Face yoga is a wellness practice, not a medical treatment. Appearance-related outcomes vary and are not guaranteed.

Is it for me?

Who is Face Yoga For?

Face yoga is for any woman who would like to care for her face in a natural, relaxing way. It's never too late to start.

  • Complete beginners, at any stage of life
  • Anyone who holds tension in the face, jaw, neck or shoulders
  • Women who want a little time just for themselves
  • Anyone curious about natural ways to support their skin and wellbeing
Do I need any experience?
No. Classes are beginner-friendly and every exercise is shown step by step, at a pace that suits you.
What should I bring?
Just bring yourself, a little time for you and a clean, make-up-free face. Everything else you need will be shared when you book.
Botox or fillers
You can still practise face yoga. Please wait at least 2 weeks after treatment and check with your practitioner. Face yoga may make the effect fade sooner than expected, so you might like to slow down around treated areas.
Pregnancy
You're welcome to join. Please let Gül know before class, as some acupressure points are best avoided during pregnancy.

Meet your teacher

Meet Gül

Hello, I'm Gül — an architect, a mum of one, a lifelong learner and the woman behind Glowdom Face Yoga.

Four years ago, I moved to East Sheen with my family, and this lovely corner of South West London has since become our home.

I've always been curious by nature. I love discovering new things, exploring different interests and challenging myself to learn something new. That's exactly how my face yoga journey began over three years ago.

What started as a little time for myself soon became something I genuinely loved. I was fascinated by how simple facial exercises, self-massage and relaxation could become part of my everyday wellbeing routine. That curiosity eventually led me to become a certified teacher of the Danielle Collins Face Yoga Method.

I believe in the power of women, but I also believe that taking care of ourselves should be part of that power.

As a woman, a mother and someone who has spent years balancing work, family and everyday responsibilities, I know how easily our own needs can slip to the bottom of the list.

That's why I created Glowdom.

I wanted to bring something uplifting to our local community — a welcoming space where women can come together, discover face yoga, share a little laughter and enjoy an hour that's entirely their own.

For me, Glowdom is about more than facial exercises. It's about feeling good in your own skin, reconnecting with yourself and remembering that looking after yourself isn't a luxury.

Because when we make time for ourselves, we have even more to give to the world around us.

I can't wait to welcome you to Glowdom.

Gül x

Good to know

Health & Wellbeing

Face yoga suits most people, and many techniques can be adapted. If you have a medical condition or concern, please read these points before you book. If your question isn't covered, feel free to get in touch.

Surgery
If you've had surgery on your face, neck or head, please check with your doctor first and allow it to heal fully before starting.
Injuries
If you have an injury to your face, neck or shoulders, please ask your doctor or practitioner for advice. In class, don't try hard and stay within your comfort zone.
Rosacea or sensitive skin
Many techniques are gentle, and lighter options are always offered. Avoid rubbing or using high pressure on affected areas.
Medical or skin conditions
Please check with your doctor before starting face yoga.
