# Putting the Privacy Notice into practice

Notice date/version: 19 October 2026. Public URL: https://www.glowdom.co.uk/privacy.html

The notice covers Gül Ayan operating Glowdom Face Yoga; consultation forms after booking and payment and before the first session; Gmail and Instagram contact; records on Gül's laptop; GitHub Pages hosting; and IONOS domain/DNS services. No payment provider is named because one has not yet been chosen. Any provider used must be explained before payment and reflected in the notice where relevant.

## Consent before receiving health information

Use `CONSULTATION-EMAIL.txt` when sending the consultation form. Give the participant the Privacy Notice before they return health information. Ask for the separate explicit health-data consent statement in the return email, alongside the completed form. A participation signature or general liability waiver is not a substitute for health-data consent.

Keep the affirmative reply, its date and the wording agreed to with the consultation record. If the participant declines, discuss the available options without asking them to send health information through Instagram. If consent is withdrawn, stop the consent-based use and address deletion unless a specific lawful reason requires limited retention.

## Record management

The notice uses purpose-based deletion criteria rather than claiming an unconfirmed fixed period is already followed:

- Enquiries: delete when answered and any requested follow-up is finished, unless a specific unresolved issue requires the relevant record.
- Consultation forms, health notes and associated consent records: review for deletion at the end of the booked session/course and any specific agreed follow-up. Stop consent-based use and address earlier deletion if consent is withdrawn.
- Necessary financial records: keep separately under the applicable UK tax/accounting rules. Individual self-employed records are normally kept at least five years after the relevant 31 January tax-return submission deadline, with longer requirements in some circumstances. This statement does not assert that Gül has already registered as self-employed or formed a company.
- Only keep limited relevant records longer for an actual legal claim, dispute or another specific lawful requirement. Record the reason.

These rules apply across Gmail and laptop copies. Check any synced folders or backups so records and providers are accounted for accurately. The website does not automatically delete Gmail, laptop or backup records. Note the end of each session/course, any agreed follow-up, and the deletion decision; do not keep health forms indefinitely for an unspecified future use.

## Security and access

Limit access to client records. Protect the laptop and Gmail account appropriately; device encryption, a strong device password, current software and multi-factor authentication are practical measures to implement. The notice does not claim those device settings have been independently verified. Never add completed client forms or client records to the public website repository.

## Rights and changes

Privacy requests go to glowdom.london@gmail.com. Normally respond within one month and explain any lawful extension. Recheck the notice before adding a payment provider, new form/storage service, marketing activity, analytics or any materially different use of information.
